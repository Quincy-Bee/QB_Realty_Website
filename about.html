THE STORY

BUILT ON EXPERIENCE.

Quincy Bee is a New York City real estate broker and the Founder and Broker of QB Realty Group. With more than a decade of experience in New York City real estate, Quincy has developed a deep understanding of the market, its neighborhoods, and the people navigating it.

His experience spans residential sales and rentals, with a particular focus on strategic representation, marketing, negotiation, and helping clients make confident real estate decisions throughout Brooklyn and New York City.

QB Realty Group was founded to bring that experience into a more personal, modern approach to real estate— combining thoughtful representation with strategic marketing, direct communication, and a strong understanding of the New York market.
