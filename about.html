ABOUT QB REALTY GROUP

New York City real estate is more than transactions—it's about navigating one of the most important decisions in a person's life with the right strategy, guidance, and representation.

For more than a decade, Quincy Bee has helped clients move through the complexities of New York City's real estate market, building a reputation for thoughtful service, strategic marketing, and dedicated advocacy. After spending 10 years with some of the city's most respected brokerages, Quincy founded QB Realty Group with a vision to create a more personalized, client-focused approach to real estate.

Whether you're preparing to sell, searching for your next home, or looking for the right rental, every move deserves a strategy—not a one-size-fits-all approach. At QB Realty Group, clients receive a combination of market expertise, modern marketing, skilled negotiation, and clear communication designed to create confident decisions from the first conversation through closing day.

For homeowners, that means developing a tailored marketing plan designed to maximize exposure, create demand, and position a property to achieve its highest potential. For buyers and renters, it means understanding individual goals, navigating New York City's competitive landscape, and securing the right opportunity with confidence.

QB Realty Group was built on the belief that exceptional representation goes beyond the transaction. It's about earning trust, building relationships, and delivering an experience defined by professionalism, transparency, and results.
