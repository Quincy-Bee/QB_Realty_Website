ABOUT QB REALTY GROUP

For more than a decade, I've helped clients navigate one of life's biggest decisions—real estate. After spending 10 years with some of New York City's most respected brokerages, I founded QB Realty Group with a simple vision: deliver a higher level of service, smarter marketing, and unwavering advocacy for every client.

Whether you're preparing to sell, searching for your next home, or looking for the right rental, every move deserves a strategy—not a one-size-fits-all approach. I combine market expertise, modern marketing, skilled negotiation, and clear communication to help my clients make confident decisions from our first conversation to closing day.

For homeowners, that means creating a tailored marketing plan designed to maximize exposure, generate demand, and position your property to achieve its highest potential. For buyers and renters, it means understanding your goals, navigating New York City's competitive market, and helping you secure the right property with confidence.

QB Realty Group was built on the belief that exceptional representation goes beyond transactions. It's about earning trust, building lasting relationships, and delivering an experience defined by professionalism, transparency, and results.

Because real estate isn't just about properties—it's about the people, dreams, and futures they represent.
